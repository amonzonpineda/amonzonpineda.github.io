05 — Process Improvement

What I Put in Place

Two systems implemented during this sprint that now serve as the standard for every future product launch.

🚀
Standardized Launch Playbook
Streamline every future launch

Built a master ClickUp sprint template — one click generates the complete 21-day sprint with all tasks, owners, dependencies, and deadlines pre-loaded.

Airtable → ClickUp live integration — when the Creative Director marks artwork complete, the Copywriter's task auto-unlocks with zero manual handoff.

All SOPs version-controlled in Box with a naming convention and quarterly review cycle to keep them current.

ClickUp Brain AI used for automated sprint setup, SOP drafting, and standup summaries — reducing PM admin time significantly.

⚡ ~60% faster launch setup · Enables PM to run 3× more launches simultaneously
🤖
AI-Powered QA System
Improve QA accuracy over time

Claude AI deployed as first-pass QA auditor — full product page copy checked against the QA checklist in under 60 seconds before any human reviews it.

Airtable automation fires Slack + email alerts if any QA checklist item is incomplete 24 hours before go-live — zero manual chasing.

Two-Eyes Rule enforced — AI is Eye 1, PM/Ops is Eye 2. Nothing goes to E-Commerce Marketplace on a single review.

Prompt template stored in Box and reused on every launch — same audit quality every time, no rebuilding from scratch.

⚡ ~70% less manual QA time · Consistent checklist · Scales to every product

07 — Retrospective

Key Lessons Learned

Key takeaways from the Sprint Retrospective — applied immediately to the next launch cycle.

LESSON 01
Single Points of Failure Must Be Identified on Day 1
The Creative Director owned 3 of 12 tasks. When they encountered a family emergency on Day 8, it nearly stalled the entire creative track. Going forward, every resource with 2+ critical tasks gets a designated backup identified at Sprint Planning.
LESSON 02
QA Starts at Briefing, Not at Delivery
Most of the 18 errors were preventable if the QA checklist had been shared with the Copy and Creative teams at kickoff. From Sprint 2 onward, the QA checklist is part of the creative brief — not a post-delivery review.
LESSON 03
Internal Compliance Notes Must Be in the Brief
The age restriction compliance note was buried in a working spreadsheet tab — not shared with the copywriter. This caused 3 of the 5 critical errors. All compliance directives now live in the ClickUp task brief itself.
LESSON 04
AI First-Pass QA Saves Hours, Not Minutes
Running the product page through Claude AI before the human QA review caught 11 of the 18 errors automatically. What used to take 3 hours of manual review now takes under 20 minutes. This is now standard on every launch.
LESSON 05
Approval Gates Need Calendar Holds, Not Reminders
The Day 17 CEO/CMO approval nearly slipped because both were traveling. Going forward, approval gates get calendar holds confirmed on Day 1 — not a Slack reminder the day before.
LESSON 06
The Sprint Template Pays Back Immediately
The second product launch (March 2025) was set up in 45 minutes using the ClickUp template — vs. 6 hours of planning for the first sprint. The investment in systematizing pays back on launch #2.
