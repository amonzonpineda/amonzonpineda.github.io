01 — Project Overview

The Challenge

Travel operations required a stronger talent pipeline for agents entering complex GDS-driven workflows. The challenge was to create a repeatable academy model that improved readiness before production while reducing dependency on agents already experienced in travel systems.

Objective
Build a Scalable Academy
Create a core training structure capable of preparing agents in travel industry basics and GDS workflows before nesting and operations handoff.

05 — Process Improvement

What I Put in Place

The academy model converted training from a reactive ramp challenge into a structured, repeatable capability for travel operations.

🚀
Structured GDS Training Path
Increase readiness before production

Created a longer training journey that added Flight School and dedicated OJT support before full operational transition.

Separated foundational travel concepts from production nesting so agents could enter operations with stronger context.

Standardized the use of specialized trainers to support Amadeus and Sabre learning tracks.

⚡ 17% higher graduation rate · 41% higher retention after graduation

Business Case + Risk Controls
Make the model scalable

Built the financial case around training cost, vendor licensing, fill rate improvement, and projected revenue benefit.

Maintained a risk and delay log for vendor onboarding, trainer readiness, procurement timing, and regional deployment.

07 — Retrospective

Lessons Learned

Key takeaways from converting a training extension into a scalable academy and business case.

LESSON 01
Training Readiness Starts Before Nesting
Agents without travel experience needed structured context before handling complex GDS scenarios. The academy reduced the gap between classroom learning and production expectations.
LESSON 02
Vendor Setup Is a Critical Path
Licensing, procurement, payment method, and vendor activation can impact launch dates as much as curriculum design. These dependencies must be tracked from day one.
LESSON 03
OJT Must Be Designed, Not Improvised
The extra OJT week gave agents a controlled transition and helped supervisors build rapport earlier, reducing avoidable ramp friction.
LESSON 04
Business Case Thinking Makes Training Scalable
Positioning the academy around graduation, retention, quality, fill rate, and revenue impact made it easier to justify expansion beyond one operation.
LESSON 05
Role-Based Governance Protects Confidentiality
Executive, training, operations, and vendor roles were enough to show clear ownership without exposing stakeholder names or company-sensitive details.
LESSON 06
The Model Becomes a Talent Pipeline
Once standardized, Flight School can be reused to build a larger pool of travel-ready agents and reduce reliance on hiring only experienced profiles.
